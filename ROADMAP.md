# Learning roadmap

## Available in hosted classrooms

Attendance → live quiz → exit ticket → topic feedback. Save and reuse lesson quizzes. Export classroom and session records.

## Next: validate with teachers

- Pilot short lessons with teachers and document onboarding friction.
- Test multilingual copy, keyboard/screen-reader use and slow connections.
- Improve question editing, imports and reusable lesson examples.

## Then: make the next lesson easier

- Teacher-selected follow-up activities for misunderstood topics.
- Accessible printable alternatives for learners without devices.
- Optional teacher-confirmed roster matching, with clear consent and correction.

## Infrastructure track

- A reproducible self-hosting distribution with account/storage adapters.
- Measured realtime transport and reconnect behavior before scale claims.
- Shared persistence and distributed limits before multi-instance hosting.

No dates or completion claims for these proposed items. Keep scope centred on learning, not rankings or surveillance.
